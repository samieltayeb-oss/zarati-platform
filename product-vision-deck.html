Challenges After The War 04 / 20
Post-War Reality · واقع ما بعد الحرب
The 2023 war has created the world's largest internal displacement crisis — and a complete agricultural data vacuum.
8.5M
Internally
Displaced
April
2023 War
Started
🏚️
Displaced Farming Families
Millions of farming families forced from their land, with no registry, no claims system, and no reconnection path.
🏗️
Destroyed Infrastructure
Gezira Scheme irrigation systems, grain storage, and rural markets severely damaged across conflict zones.
📦
Supply Chain Collapse
Agricultural input supply networks broken. Seed, fertilizer, and pesticide access near-zero in many states.
🏪
Market Access Breakdown
Key commodity markets in Khartoum destroyed. Farmers selling at extreme discounts to local brokers with no alternatives.
🏦
Financial System Disruption
Banking system largely non-functional in conflict zones. Agricultural credit and insurance entirely unavailable.
📊
Agricultural Data Vacuum
No real-time crop data, no national yield visibility, no early warning system. Policy is made blind. Aid is distributed inefficiently.
Source: OCHA Sudan Situation Reports, 2024
