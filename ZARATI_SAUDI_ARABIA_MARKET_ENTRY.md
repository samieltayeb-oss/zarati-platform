## 2. Institutional Landscape & Regulatory Ecosystem

### 2.1 Statutory & Sovereign Entities
1. **Ministry of Environment, Water and Agriculture (MEWA / وزارة البيئة والمياه والزراعة):** Formulates national water conservation regulations (Royal Decree No. 66 banning high-water forage crops) and oversees domestic agricultural subsidies.
2. **General Food Security Authority (GFSA / الهيئة العامة للأمن الغذائي):** Formerly SAGO; manages national strategic grain silos, wheat import tenders, and flour milling quotas.
3. **SALIC (Saudi Agricultural and Livestock Investment Company):** The agricultural investment arm of the Public Investment Fund (PIF), mandated to secure overseas agricultural supply chains across Ukraine, Australia, Brazil, and East Africa.
4. **Agricultural Development Fund (ADF / صندوق التنمية الزراعية):** Provides government financing for high-tech greenhouse projects and overseas contract farming loans.

### 2.2 Cybersecurity & Sovereign Cloud Compliance
Operating in the Kingdom requires strict adherence to regulatory standards:
* **Digital Government Authority (DGA / هيئة الحكومة الرقمية):** Enforces government enterprise architecture standards and platform interoperability.
* **National Cybersecurity Authority (NCA / الهيئة الوطنية للأمن السيبراني):** Requires compliance with **Essential Cybersecurity Controls (ECC-1:2018)** and Cloud Cybersecurity Controls (CCC-1:2020).
* **Communications, Space and Technology Commission (CST / هيئة الاتصالات والفضاء والتقنية):** Enforces the **Cloud Computing Regulatory Framework (CCRF)**:
  - Government agricultural data and national food reserve statistics are classified as **Government Restricted (Class B or Class C)**, legally mandating that all data at rest and in transit must reside strictly on in-Kingdom data centers (e.g., Oracle Cloud Riyadh/Jeddah, stc cloud, or Google Cloud Dammam).

## 4. Primary Target Buyers in the Kingdom

1. **SALIC & Sovereign Investment Funds:**
   - *Pain Point:* SALIC manages billions in overseas farm assets but lacks ground-truth verification of private grain contracting in the Nile Basin.
   - *Zarati Offering:* "Zarati Sovereign Supply Radar"—satellite and field registry verification of contract farming schemes in Eastern Sudan.
2. **Major Saudi Agribusiness Conglomerates:**
   - **Almarai & NADEC:** Massive demand for green animal fodder (alfalfa/Rhodes grass) imported from Sudan following the domestic water extraction ban. Zarati provides verified contract tracking for fodder cultivation along the Northern State Nile schemes.
   - **Savola Group & Al-Kasih / Halwani Bros:** Primary processors of sesame for tahina and halawa. Zarati provides direct source-level procurement intelligence from the Gedaref Crop Exchange.
   - **ARASCO (Arabian Agricultural Services Company):** Largest feed manufacturer in the Middle East; requires massive predictable sorghum and corn imports.
