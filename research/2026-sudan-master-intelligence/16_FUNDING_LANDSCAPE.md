## 3. Structured Sudanese Diaspora Capital Mobilization

Sudan possesses an extraordinarily prosperous and educated diaspora residing in the Gulf Cooperation Council (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar), Canada, the United States, and the United Kingdom, remitting an estimated **$3B to $4B annually**:

### "Zarati Diaspora Scheme" Architecture:
1. **The Problem:** Diaspora professionals desire to invest in productive Sudanese farmland and solar irrigation schemes but cannot trust distant family members or unverified local contractors.
2. **The Zarati Solution:** Diaspora syndicates invest working capital into vetted farmer cooperatives via Zarati. Zarati provides continuous **Sentinel-2 satellite NDVI crop growth verification**, periodic drone footage, and Bankak escrow dividend distributions back to Canadian or Gulf bank accounts.
3. **Regulatory Protection:** Structured through a Canadian holding entity (Calgary, Alberta) adhering to FINTRAC and international AML/KYC compliance standards.
