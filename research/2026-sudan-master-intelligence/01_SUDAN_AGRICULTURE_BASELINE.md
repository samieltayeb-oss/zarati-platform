## 1. Macroeconomic Contributions & Demographic Baselines

  * **Value:** **30.0% to 34.5%** of Real GDP `[VERIFIED FACT]`.

## 2. Land Allocation & Production Zones

### 2.1 Arable Land Reserve vs. Actual Cultivated Acreage
* **Total Arable Land Endowment:**
  * **Value:** **175.0 million feddans** (~73.5 million hectares) officially recognized; broader potential agricultural land cited up to **200.0 to 210.0 million feddans** (~84.0 to 88.2 million ha) `[VERIFIED FACT]`.
  * **Source:** FAO Country Strategic Plan; Sudan Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry.
* **Pre-Conflict Cultivated Footprint (Typical Season):**
  * **Value:** **30.0 to 40.0 million feddans** (~12.6 to 16.8 million ha), meaning Sudan actively cultivated only **17% to 23%** of its arable potential `[VERIFIED FACT]`.
  * **Source:** FAO CFSAM Historical Time Series (2018–2022).
* **Conflict-Era Cultivated Acreage:**
  * *2023 Season:* Collapsed to **18.0 – 22.0 million feddans** (-35% decline due to diesel collapse, conflict, and bank closures) `[ESTIMATE]`.
  * *2024 Season:* Rebounded to **25.0 – 28.0 million feddans** due to massive expansion in Gedaref, Kassala, and River Nile `[ESTIMATE]`.
  * *2025/2026 Season:* Retracted to **22.0 – 24.5 million feddans** following conflict spread into Sennar and Al Jazirah `[ESTIMATE]`.

## 4. National Food Balance, Imports & Post-Harvest Losses

### 4.1 Cereal Consumption Deficit & Import Dependency
* **National Cereal Demand:** **7.6 to 8.2 million metric tonnes** required annually for human consumption, livestock feed, seed reserves, and processing `[ESTIMATE]`.
* **The Wheat Gap:** Domestic production (433k MT) supplies only **17% to 20%** of demand. Sudan imports **1.7 to 2.1 million MT of wheat annually**, primarily through Port Sudan from Black Sea/Russian ports `[VERIFIED FACT]`.
* **Food Security Emergency (IPC Phase 3+):** Over **19.5 million Sudanese (40%+ of population)** suffer acute crisis-level food insecurity in 2026, with active famine conditions verified in North Darfur (Zamzam IDP camp) `[VERIFIED FACT]`.

### 4.2 Post-Harvest Losses (PHL) Reality
* **Official FAO Accounting Rate:** 5.0% flat allowance for sorghum/millet `[ESTIMATE]`.
* **Empirical Field Reality:** **20.0% to 35.0%** cumulative loss in traditional underground pits (*Matamir*) and elevated thatch cribs (*Souba*) `[VERIFIED FACT]`.
  * *Primary Vectors:* Khapra Beetle (*Trogoderma granarium*), water table seepage causing aflatoxin fungal rot, and wartime transport blockades preventing grain movement.

## 5. Livestock Assets & Pastoral Economy

* **National Herd Inventory:** **105.6 to 115.4 million head** `[ESTIMATE]`.
  * **Sheep (ضأن):** 39.8 – 42.9 million (Desert breeds: Hamari, Kabashi, Dubasi).
  * **Goats (ماعز):** 31.0 – 33.8 million.
  * **Cattle (أبقار):** 30.1 – 33.5 million (Baggara Zebu, Butana, Kenana).
  * **Camels (إبل):** 4.7 – 5.1 million (Dromedary: Anafi racing, Rashaidi pack).
* **Export Outlets:** Live sheep exported to Saudi Arabia via Suakin / Port Sudan; camels driven along the *Arba'een* desert trail to southern Egypt (Daraw auction).
