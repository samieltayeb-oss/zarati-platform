## 3. Justification of Market Priorities

### 1. South Sudan (Score: 85.0 — Priority 1)
- **The Renk–Kosti Grain Artery:** Renk (Upper Nile State, South Sudan) is physically contiguous with White Nile and Sennar states. Large mechanized sorghum and sesame schemes in Renk have historically been operated by Sudanese scheme managers and financed through Sudanese cross-border trade houses.
- **Linguistic Synergy:** Juba Arabic is the national lingua franca; business is conducted in Arabic and English.
- **Zero Competitor Presence:** No major Kenyan or Nigerian agritech platform has successfully scaled in South Sudan. Zarati faces an open competitive greenfield.

### 2. Chad (Score: 71.5 — Priority 2)
- **Ecological Continuity:** Eastern Chad (Ouaddaï, Sila) mirrors West Darfur in soil composition, rainfall, and crops (gum arabic, millet, sesame, cattle).
- **Chadian Arabic:** Arabic is an official language and the commercial lingua franca of cattle and grain markets across Chad.

### 3. Kenya (Score: 54.0 — Deprioritize)
- **Hyper-Competitive Red Ocean:** Kenya is heavily saturated with well-funded incumbents (Twiga, Apollo, DigiFarm, Pula, Hello Tractor). Customer acquisition cost (CAC) is extraordinarily high, and margins are compressed. Zarati should not enter Kenya.
