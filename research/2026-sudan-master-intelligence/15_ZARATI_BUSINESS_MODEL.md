## 1. Multi-Stream Revenue Architecture

To ensure operational profitability without burdening vulnerable smallholders, Zarati deploys a tiered, multi-sided B2B monetization model:

| Revenue Stream | Target Customer | Pricing Structure | Estimated Unit Contribution | Margin Profile |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **1. B2B Trader Pro Subscriptions** | Wholesale grain merchants, exporters, agro-processors | **$45 / month** (or ~150k SDG) | Direct SaaS recurring revenue | 90%+ gross margin |
| **2. Enterprise Exporter Suite** | Large trading houses (DAL Group, CTC Group, Mahgoub Sons) | **$350 / month** + custom volume | Custom procurement analytics, bulk escrow | 85%+ gross margin |
| **3. Marketplace Settlement Brokerage** | Transaction parties closing via Zarati Escrow | **1.5% – 2.5% take-rate** on closed transaction volume | Paid by buyer/seller upon successful weighbridge release | 75% net margin after bank rail fees |
| **4. Institutional NGO M&E Suite** | FAO, WFP, IFAD, international relief agencies | **$1.50 per registered smallholder** per agricultural season | Satellite proof-of-cultivation & input audit | 80%+ gross margin |
| **5. Input Supplier Sponsored Placement** | Certified seed producers, solar pump dealers, fertilizer distributors | **$500 – $2,000 / month** per brand | Featured category listings, sponsored advisory cards | 95% gross margin |
