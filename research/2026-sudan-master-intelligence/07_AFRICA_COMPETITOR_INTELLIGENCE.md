### Strategic Takeaways for Zarati:
1. **Never Replicate Twiga or iProcure:** Both failed by owning physical trucks, renting high-cost regional warehouses, and carrying massive inventory risk. Zarati must operate as an **asset-light intelligence broker**, matching buyers and sellers and facilitating escrow via Bankak without taking physical possession of grain.
2. **Replicate Farmerline's Vernacular Focus:** Farmerline won in Ghana because it spoke local dialects via voice. Zarati must deliver **Arabic-first, Sudanese colloquial dialect-ready** interfaces (audio messages, WhatsApp bots, and voice-assisted web components).
3. **Capture the AFEX Opportunity Digitally:** Sudan lacks a modernized commodity exchange. Gedaref and El Obeid operate on manual chalkboards. Zarati can become Sudan's **de facto digital commodity exchange** by issuing cryptographically verifiable digital lot certificates without spending millions on concrete silos.
