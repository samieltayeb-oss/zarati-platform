## 1. Institutional Landscape & Public Sector Realities

Sudan's agricultural bureaucracy has historically managed massive state-run schemes, including the Gezira Scheme (2.2M feddans), Rahad Scheme (300k feddans), and New Halfa (400k feddans). However, administrative disruption since April 2023 caused the loss of physical archives, land records, and statistical capabilities across federal ministries.

Key government bodies operating from temporary administrative headquarters (Port Sudan, Gedaref, Atbara, and Dongola):
1. **Federal Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry (MOAF):** Retains statutory oversight over national agricultural policy, plant quarantine, national grain balances, and bilateral ag-trade pacts.
2. **Agricultural Research Corporation (ARC - Wad Medani / Gedaref):** Premier scientific institution responsible for certified seed breeding, soil mapping, and agronomic recommendations.
3. **State Ministries of Production & Economic Resources:** Direct operational authority over local markets, auction taxes, crop cess collection, and scheme tenant registrations in states like Gedaref, Kassala, and River Nile.
4. **Strategic Reserve Corporation (البنك الزراعي السوداني / المخزون الاستراتيجي):** Responsible for domestic food security buffer stocks, historically financed through the Agricultural Bank of Sudan (ABS).

## 2. Institutional Pain Points & Zarati Solutions

| Public Sector Pain Point | Pre-Conflict Practice | Current Disrupted State | Zarati B2G Solution | Strategic Value to State |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Farmer Registry & Tenant Records** | Paper ledger books stored in scheme offices | Destroyed, looted, or inaccessible in contested zones | **Digital Farmer Registry (DFR)**: Cloud-native, encrypted tenant database with Feddan polygons | Restores formal tenant verification, inheritance rights, and land use mapping |
| **Harvest Yield Estimation** | Manual multi-stage field crop cut surveys by extension agents | Impossible due to field insecurity and fuel shortages | **Remote Sensing Yield Forecasting Engine**: Blends Sentinel-2 NDVI with historical ARC yield curves | Real-time state and locality yield predictions 4–6 weeks ahead of harvest |
| **Market Cess & Auction Tax Leakage** | Physical cash collection at checkpoint gates and market barriers | Heavy cash leakage, informal bypassing, extortion | **Digital Market Gate & Auction Clearing Ledger**: QR code transaction verification via Bankak | Increases formal state municipal tax revenue while removing physical checkpoint graft |
| **Emergency Seed Distribution Tracking** | Manual paper distribution lists prone to diversion | Inability to audit whether emergency seed reached actual smallholders | **Biometric / SMS Voucher Redemption Tracking**: Real-time distribution auditing | Restores multilateral donor confidence for funding government programs |

## 3. Neutral Institutional Positioning & Governance Protocols

Operating agricultural infrastructure in a complex transitional environment requires rigorous institutional neutrality:

1. **Strict Data Sovereignty & Encryption:**
   - Farmer personal identifying information (PII) and GPS farm coordinates are protected under zero-knowledge enterprise encryption.
   - Farm boundary polygons are shared with public authorities only in aggregated, anonymized 10-km² statistical blocks to prevent targeting or exploitation.
2. **Multi-Stakeholder Steering Committee:**
   - Zarati governance includes an Advisory Council comprising veteran Sudanese agronomists (former ARC directors), University of Gezira and University of Khartoum faculty, and recognized civil society farmer cooperative leaders.
3. **Open Standards & Non-Exclusivity:**
   - B2G modules adhere to open geospatial standards (OGC, GeoJSON, STAC), ensuring that public datasets can be exported to international bodies (FAO, IFAD) without vendor lock-in.
