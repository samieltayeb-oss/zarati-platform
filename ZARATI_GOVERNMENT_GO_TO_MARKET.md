## 3. The 4-Phase B2G Procurement Pathway

1. **Step 1: The Ministerial Briefing & Live Simulation (Port Sudan):**
   - Demonstrate the National Command Center prototype using historical Gedaref data.
   - Deliver the *Sovereign Data Security & Data Residency Guarantee Brief*.
2. **Step 2: Sponsoring State Pilot Decree (Gedaref State):**
   - State Ministry of Production issues an administrative decree authorizing the 6-month Gedaref Flagship Pilot across 3 localities.
   - Deployment funded via an existing multilateral technical assistance grant (e.g., AfDB Emergency Food Production Facility).
3. **Step 3: Joint Field Implementation & Extension Training:**
   - Onboard 50 state agricultural extension officers as certified Zarati Field Supervisors.
   - Conduct hands-on digital mapping across 10 pilot farmer cooperatives.
4. **Step 4: National Cabinet Ratification & Multi-State Rollout:**
   - Present audited pilot results (increased farmer price realization, tax audit compliance) to the Council of Ministers.
   - Sign 3-year National Software License covering all 18 states.

## 4. Civil Service Capacity Building & Transfer Plan

Zarati rejects the model of foreign technology black-boxes that collapse when external consultants depart. Zarati establishes a comprehensive **Sovereign Technology Transfer Framework**:
* **The Zarati Ag-Cadre Academy:** A 4-week structured certification curriculum for Ministry of Agriculture civil servants, training them in PostGIS spatial analysis, satellite NDVI interpretation, and market anomaly investigation.
* **Open Geospatial Formats:** All spatial layers export to standard OGC formats (GeoJSON, Shapefile, GeoTIFF), guaranteeing that government agronomists can continue utilizing data in QGIS or standard ESRI software without vendor lock-in.
