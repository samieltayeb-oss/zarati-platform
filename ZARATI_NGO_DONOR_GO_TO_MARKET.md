## 1. The Multilateral Humanitarian Challenge in Sudan

International development finance and humanitarian agencies commit hundreds of millions of dollars to Sudanese agriculture annually:
* **FAO Emergency Agriculture Response:** Target of reaching 1.5 million rural farming households (7.5 million people) with certified sorghum, millet, and sesame seeds across 17 states in 2025/2026 `[VERIFIED FACT]`.
* **WFP Emergency Food Assistance & Local Procurement:** Massive ongoing grain procurement and food basket distribution.
* **AfDB Sudan Emergency Wheat Production Project:** \$34M grant administered to support irrigated winter wheat in Northern and River Nile states `[VERIFIED FACT]`.
* **IFAD & Islamic Development Bank (IsDB):** Multi-year smallholder resilience and irrigation rehabilitation funds.

### The Fatal Operational Bottleneck:
In a conflict-disrupted environment with fragmented security checkpoints, donors face acute operational risks:
1. **Aid Diversion & Leakage:** Inability to prove that subsidized seed packs reached registered smallholders rather than being diverted to commercial grain markets.
2. **Ghost Beneficiaries:** Duplicate manual paper registration lists resulting in double-counting across overlapping NGO programs.
3. **M&E Blind Spots:** Impossible to verify whether distributed seeds were successfully planted or failed due to localized rainfall drought, as field travel is restricted for expatriate staff.

**Zarati solves this through Auditable Digital Agricultural Telemetry.**

## 3. Targeted Institutional Value Propositions

### 3.1 For UN FAO (Emergency Seeds & Smallholder Resilience)
* **Value:** Zarati's Digital Farmer Registry provides FAO field officers with verified farm polygons and real-time seed distribution tracking, restoring international donor audit compliance and accelerating emergency funding cycles.

### 3.2 For UN WFP (Local Food Procurement & Market Monitoring)
* **Value:** WFP's mandate to "buy local" often sparks unintended local grain price spikes. Zarati's multi-tier price discovery engine provides daily market liquidity indices, allowing WFP procurement directors to buy sorghum in Gedaref without destabilizing domestic retail grain prices.

### 3.3 For World Bank & African Development Bank (AfDB)
* **Value:** Multilateral infrastructure grants require verified project baseline and endline evaluations. Zarati provides verifiable multi-year time series showing feddan expansion, yield improvement, and household income growth.
