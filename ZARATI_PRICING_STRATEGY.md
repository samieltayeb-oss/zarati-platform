## 1. Commercial Pricing Manifesto

1. **Farmers Never Pay for Core Access:** Smallholder farmers and scheme tenants pay zero subscription fees, zero registration fees, and zero listing fees for standard grain and oilseed sales. Monetization is pushed entirely to institutional and commercial beneficiaries.
2. **Value-Based B2G Structuring:** Government pricing is benchmarked against the direct physical tax leakage prevented at municipal crop auction gates, and the multi-million-dollar cost savings achieved by replacing manual paper surveys with automated satellite yield forecasts.
3. **Hard-Currency Institutional Contracts:** All government, donor, and enterprise contracts are denominated in US Dollars (USD) or Saudi Riyals (SAR) to insulate Zarati from local currency volatility.

## 3. Commercial Rationale Across Geographic Tiers

### 3.1 Sudan Flagship Market (Post-Conflict Reconstruction)
* **Strategy:** Penetration-focused B2G pricing with multilateral co-sponsorship.
* **Funding Mechanism:** State pilots and national command center deployments are structured to be funded through multilateral reconstruction grants (World Bank Emergency Agrifood Program, African Development Bank, Islamic Development Bank).
* **Return on Investment for State:** In Gedaref State alone, a 1% reduction in municipal auction tax evasion across 5 million bags of sorghum recovers over **\$1.2 Million USD annually**, delivering a $>8\times\text{ ROI}$ on the Zarati state software license in Year 1.

### 3.2 Pan-African Expansion Markets (Kenya, Ethiopia, Chad)
* **Strategy:** Standard commercial GovTech licensing bundled with telecom revenue-sharing (e.g., Safaricom in Kenya, Ethio Telecom in Ethiopia).

### 3.3 Gulf & Saudi Arabia Market (High-Value Sovereign Provenance)
* **Strategy:** Enterprise B2B SaaS sold to sovereign off-takers (SALIC, Silal) and commercial agribusinesses (Almarai, Savola).
* **Pricing Model:** Premium tier (\$250k - \$500k/yr) reflecting the massive strategic value of securing guaranteed non-GMO grain and livestock import quotas.
