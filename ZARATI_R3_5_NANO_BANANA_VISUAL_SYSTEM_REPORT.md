## 3. Master Nano Banana Visual Language

- **Aesthetic:** High-end documentary photography, Sudanese agricultural realism, institutional editorial photography, cinematic but natural, restrained.
- **Lighting & Optics:** Full-frame camera sensors, natural sun angles (golden dawn, high sun with natural contrast, late afternoon vertisol glow), shallow depth of field on crop morphology, wide angle for panoramic irrigation corridors.
- **Palette Alignment:** Deep Nile (`#061D38`), Acacia Gold (`#D4AF37`), Agricultural Green (`#2E7D32`), Clay Vertisol Earth (`#2D241E`), Parchment (`#F9F6F0`).
- **Strict Exclusions Enforced:** Zero camels, zero pyramids, zero desert sand dunes, zero poverty tropes, zero generic American/European farms, zero synthetic AI text or fake government stamps.
