## 1. Methodology & Weighted Scoring Framework

To prevent emotional or opportunistic geographic expansion, Zarati applies a multi-factor quantitative scoring model across 22 target nations.

### Evaluation Dimensions (100-Point Weighted Index):
1. **Agricultural Scale & Arable Endowment (15%):** Total arable hectares, national ag GDP share, export volume.
2. **Government Digitization Need & Data Void (15%):** Severity of paper-based administrative gaps; absence of sovereign digital agriculture infrastructure.
3. **Multilateral Donor Ag-Funding Presence (15%):** Capital flows from FAO, WFP, World Bank, IFAD, AfDB, and IsDB.
4. **Linguistic & Regulatory Affinity (10%):** Arabic language native support, civil law/common law compatibility, regional trade pacts (COMESA, GAFTA).
5. **Cross-Border Trade Linkage to Sudan / Red Sea (10%):** Direct import/export corridors, maritime routes, or shared transboundary river basins.
6. **Commercial Willingness to Pay (10%):** Sovereign wealth fund budgets, enterprise agribusiness balance sheets, ministry IT budgets.
7. **Telecom & Digital Payment Readiness (10%):** Mobile money penetration, 3G/4G coverage, smartphone adoption.
8. **Competitive White Space (10%):** Absence of entrenched, well-capitalized sovereign Agri-OS competitors.
9. **Political & Operational Stability (5%):** Rule of law, physical safety, ease of international capital repatriation.

## 4. Expansion Criteria & Go/No-Go Decision Gate

Zarati will enter a new national market if and only if **all four gate conditions** are satisfied:
1. **Institutional Sponsor Identified:** Formal bilateral agreement with a national Ministry of Agriculture, sovereign food security authority (e.g., SALIC, GFSA, Silal), or multilateral lead agency (e.g., FAO, World Bank).
2. **Country Pack Configured:** Declarative Country Pack JSON completed, defining local administrative boundaries, customary units, and local payment integration.
3. **Data Residency Compliance Guaranteed:** In-country sovereign cloud hosting verified under local national cybersecurity statutes.
4. **Funding Secured:** Contractual deployment licensing fees or development grant funding contracted to cover 100% of national localization and operational overhead.
