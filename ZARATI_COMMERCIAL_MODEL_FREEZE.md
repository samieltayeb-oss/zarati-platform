# ZARATI COMMERCIAL MODEL FREEZE

## The Anti-Hype Commercial Reality

The R2.5 strategy documents explored numerous theoretical monetization avenues. This document freezes the commercial model to the realistic, near-term pathways that do not rely on unbuilt technology or assumed market dominance.

## Primary Revenue Streams (Focus)

1.  **SaaS Subscriptions (Traders & Agribusinesses):**
    *   **Reality:** The primary near-term revenue driver. Traders pay for premium discovery tools, advanced filtering, and market intelligence reports (post-R4).
    *   **Status:** Requires R3 and R4 implementation.

2.  **Government / NGO Data Licensing (Sovereign Model):**
    *   **Reality:** Zarati licenses aggregated, anonymized agricultural data (production estimates, pricing trends) to ministries and development organizations.
    *   **Status:** Highly dependent on achieving scale in the Gedaref pilot and successfully building R4 data pipelines. Requires strict adherence to the Data Sovereignty Policy.

## Deprioritized / Excluded Revenue Streams

1.  **Transaction Fees (Take-Rate):**
    *   **Status: EXCLUDED.** Zarati does not process payments or manage escrow. Attempting to extract a percentage of off-platform, cash-settled transactions is impossible to enforce and introduces massive regulatory/compliance risk.

2.  **Logistics Commission:**
    *   **Status: EXCLUDED.** Zarati is not a freight broker.

3.  **Financial Product Lead Gen (Credit/Insurance):**
    *   **Status: DEFERRED (VISION).** While theoretically possible, this requires a mature ecosystem and proven data reliability that Zarati does not yet possess.

## Financial Modeling Constraints

*   Financial projections must not assume rapid, viral adoption.
*   Models must account for high Customer Acquisition Costs (CAC) in rural, low-connectivity environments.
*   Revenue projections in USD must accurately reflect the complexities of operating in a highly volatile FX environment (SDG).
