## 4. Arabic Typography, RTL Geometry & Cultural Rigor

### 4.1 Typography Standards
* **Primary Arabic Display Font:** **IBM Plex Sans Arabic** or **Tajawal** (exceptional optical balance across bold headings and tiny tabular figures).
* **Tabular Figures:** Numbers formatted with dedicated tabular alignment to ensure statistical financial columns align perfectly in RTL without jitter.
* **Mixed-Direction Strings:** Phone numbers, dates, and currency abbreviations (`SDG`, `USD`) wrapped with strict Unicode directional isolating characters (`<bdi>` and `dir="ltr"`) to prevent reversed bracket errors common in poor Arabic web development.

### 4.2 Cultural Nuance & Vernacular Accuracy
* Translations strictly use authentic Sudanese agricultural vocabulary:
  - *Feddan (فدان)* instead of Hectare or Acre.
  - *Ardeb (إردب)* for sorghum/millet instead of Bushel.
  - *Qintar (قنطار)* for sesame and cotton.
  - *Hawasha (حواشة)* for scheme tenant parcels.
  - *Matmura (مطمورة)* for traditional underground grain storage.
  - *Dallal (دلال)* for licensed traditional auction broker.
