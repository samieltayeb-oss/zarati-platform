# ZARATI PRODUCT ROADMAP v2 (Post-R2.6 Freeze)

This roadmap replaces all previous timelines and strategic projections, grounding future development in the technical reality established during the R2.6 Founder Architecture Freeze.

## Phase R1 & R2: Foundation (COMPLETED)
*   **Status:** `LIVE`
*   **Achievements:** Vercel deployment, Supabase infrastructure, Multi-role Auth, Email verification, RLS, Rate Limiting, Internationalization routing.

## Phase R2.6: Architecture Freeze (CURRENT)
*   **Status:** `IN PROGRESS` (Closing)
*   **Achievements:** Documentation of implementation truth, scope locking for R3/R4, deflation of strategic claims.

## Phase R3: Marketplace Discovery Foundation (UPCOMING)
*   **Goal:** Enable digital signaling of supply (Farmers) and demand (Traders).
*   **Key Deliverables:**
    *   Database schema updates for Listings and RFQs.
    *   Farmer UI: Listing creation and management.
    *   Trader UI: Search, filter, and RFQ submission.
    *   Admin UI: Basic moderation tools.
*   **Constraint:** Zero transactional or payment functionality.

## Gedaref Pilot
*   **Goal:** Field test the platform in a constrained environment.
*   **Stages:**
    1.  Controlled Alpha (Stability focus)
    2.  Expansion Beta (Discovery focus)
    3.  Broad Rollout (Scale focus)
*   **Constraint:** Stage 1 field activation may prepare during R3, but any pilot function depending on real market intelligence must wait until the relevant R4 capability exists. The complete pilot does NOT run in parallel with unfinished R3/R4 dependencies.

## Phase R4: Market Intelligence Integration (FUTURE)
*   **Goal:** Transition from mock data to verified external intelligence.
*   **Key Deliverables:**
    *   R4-A: Institutional / Historical Data Foundation.
    *   R4-B: Weather & Automated External Feeds.
    *   R4-C: FX & Unit Normalization.
    *   R4-D: Sudan Market Reporter & Verification Foundation.
    *   R4-E: Trader & Institutional Analytics.

## Phase R5+: Advanced Ecosystem (VISION)
*   *Items in this phase are strategic goals, not committed technical requirements.*
*   National Command Center integration.
*   Advanced AI/GIS satellite monitoring.
*   Ecosystem partnerships (Financial services).
