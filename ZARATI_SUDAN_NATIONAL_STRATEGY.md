## 1. National Agro-Ecological Baseline & Strategic Significance

### 1.1 Macroeconomic Livelihood Footprint
* **Pre-Conflict GDP Contribution:** 30.0% to 34.5% of national real GDP `[VERIFIED FACT]`.
* **Conflict-Era Shock (2024–2026):** Direct agricultural value-added contracted by ~33.6%, though agriculture's relative economic importance increased to over 60% of remaining productive activity as urban manufacturing and service sectors collapsed `[ESTIMATE]`.
* **Employment & Subsistence Dependence:** An estimated 65.0% to 80.0% of the active labor force depends directly or indirectly on farming, pastoral livestock rearing, primary agricultural processing, or crop transport `[ESTIMATE]`.
* **Arable Land Endowment:** 175.0 million feddans (~73.5 million hectares) of recognized arable soils, with historical average seasonal cultivation fluctuating between 30.0 and 40.0 million feddans (~17% to 23% utilization) `[VERIFIED FACT]`.
* **Conflict Cultivation Collapse:** Cultivated footprint dropped to ~22.0 to 25.0 million feddans in the 2025/2026 seasons, creating an acute structural national cereal deficit of 2.4 to 3.0 million metric tonnes `[ESTIMATE]`.

### 2.1 State-by-State Strategic Mapping

#### Gedaref State (ولاية القضارف) — *The Flagship Anchor*
* **Cultivated Area:** 6.5 to 7.5 million feddans semi-mechanized rainfed farming `[VERIFIED FACT]`.
* **Primary Output:** Over 50% to 70% of national sorghum exports; premier producer of export-grade white sesame.
* **Institutional Center:** Gedaref Crop Exchange (سوق المحاصيل القضارف), the largest physical grain auction in East Africa.
* **Zarati Role:** Ideal pilot venue. High tractor density, commercial farmers, operational telecom infrastructure, and high appetite for digital market clearing.

#### Al Jazirah State (ولاية الجزيرة) — *The Historic Heartlands*
* **Cultivated Area:** Gezira Scheme encompasses 2.2 million feddans under gravity canal irrigation `[VERIFIED FACT]`.
* **Status:** Suffered deep administrative and physical disruptions from December 2023 through 2025. Northern blocks (El Kamlin, 24 El Qurashi) are gradually stabilizing in 2026.
* **Zarati Role:** Tenant registry digitizer. Restores lost land tenant rosters (*Hawashat*), canal water distribution monitoring, and winter wheat input tracking.

#### River Nile & Northern States (ولايتي نهر النيل والشمالية) — *The Secure Winter Cereal Shield*
* **System:** River pump lift schemes along the fertile Nile terrace soils (*Jorouf* and *Gereif*).
* **Primary Output:** 70%+ of domestic winter wheat, faba beans (*Ful Masri*), onions, alfalfa, and date palms.
* **Strategic Value:** Highly secure, hosting thousands of relocated ag-businesses and banking headquarters in Atbara, Shendi, and Dongola.

#### North & South Kordofan (شمال وجنوب كردفان) — *The Cash Crop & Livestock Belt*
* **System:** Traditional rainfed cultivation on red sand dunes (*Qoz*).
* **Primary Output:** Groundnuts (*Ful Sudani*), Gum Arabic (*Hashab* and *Talha* from the Acacia senegal belt), and Karkadeh (Hibiscus).
* **Institutional Center:** El Obeid Crop Exchange (بورصة الأبيض للمحاصيل).
* **Strategic Value:** Ground zero for smallholder export crops, but heavily constrained by road transit blockades and lack of formal physical banking.

#### Red Sea State (ولاية البحر الأحمر) — *The Export & Import Funnel*
* **Logistics Anchor:** Port Sudan Port and Suakin Livestock Harbor.
* **Primary Output:** Terminal storage, quarantine facilities, international bulk vessel discharging (wheat, urea), and live animal export to the Gulf (Jeddah, Jizan).
* **Zarati Role:** Export corridor pricing, customs clearing telemetry, FOB reference monitoring.

## 4. The Flagship Pilot Blueprint: Gedaref State

### 4.1 Why Gedaref Must Be State Zero
1. **Security Stability:** Gedaref remains firmly under stable civil administration with continuous access to Port Sudan via the Eastern Highway.
2. **Economic Scale:** Hosts ~7 million feddans of semi-mechanized production and hundreds of thousands of smallholders, generating the bulk of national grain trade.
3. **Structured Exchange:** The Gedaref Crop Market already enforces daily auctions, providing a physical anchor for digitizing spot transactions.
4. **Telecom Penetration:** High penetration of 4G networks and Starlink commercial hubs across farming hubs (El Hawata, El Gadarif, Doka).

### 4.2 Pilot Scope & Parameters
* **Target Duration:** 6 Months (spanning planting through harvest).
* **Target Users:** 5,000 Smallholder & Scheme Farmers, 150 Verified Crop Traders, 10 Cooperative Unions.
* **Target Crops:** Sorghum (Feterita & Dabar), White Sesame, Sunflower.
* **Core Modules Deployed:**
  1. Digital Farmer Registry (DFR) with GPS farm boundary mapping.
  2. Gedaref Daily Spot Price Ingestion & Bilingual SMS/App Feed.
  3. Hyper-local Weather & Rainfall Anomaly Alerts (Open-Meteo & ECMWF).
  4. Gedaref State Ministry of Production Executive Command Dashboard.
* **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):**
  - $>80\%$ daily active farmer engagement during critical agronomic decision windows.
  - Zero cross-tenant data leaks or RLS violations.
  - 100% price data availability with verified audit trail.
  - Measurable reduction in farmer price asymmetry (demonstrated $>8\%$ higher farm-gate price realization).

## 5. Reconstruction & Strategic Roadmap: 2026–2030

1. **Immediate Stabilization (Year 1):** Deploy Gedaref Pilot, launch National Command Center prototype with MOAF in Port Sudan, integrate daily WFP/FEWS NET feeds.
2. **Eastern & Northern Expansion (Year 2):** Scale to Kassala, River Nile, and Northern States; partner with Agricultural Bank of Sudan for digital Salam credit scoring.
3. **Central Schemes Rehabilitation (Year 3):** Re-digitize the Gezira and Rahad schemes as physical reconstruction takes hold; integrate satellite canal water flow monitoring.
4. **Western Integration (Year 4–5):** Expand to Kordofan and Darfur traditional belts via SMS/USSD offline channels as security normalizes.
