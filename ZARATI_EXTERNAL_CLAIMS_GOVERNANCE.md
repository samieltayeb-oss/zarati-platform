# ZARATI EXTERNAL CLAIMS GOVERNANCE

## The Anti-Hype Directive

Zarati communications—whether investor pitches, government proposals, marketing materials, or internal strategy documents—must adhere to strict standards of transparency and realism. The platform's value proposition must rely on demonstrable capabilities and verifiable data, not hyperbole.

## Prohibited Terminology

The following terms and concepts are **BANNED** from all Zarati materials unless accompanied by irrefutable, audited proof:

| Banned Phrase / Concept | Reason | Allowed Alternative |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| "The only platform..." | Impossible to verify; arrogant. | "A differentiated platform designed for..." |
| "Unassailable moat" | Business reality is dynamic; moats are built over years. | "Strategic advantages in [specific area]..." |
| "Guaranteed returns/profits" | Agriculture is volatile; Zarati cannot guarantee outcomes. | "Designed to improve access to competitive pricing..." |
| "Massive scale" | Ambiguous; implies current state rather than future goal. | "Targeting scale across [specific region/demographic]..." |
| "AI-driven" (when describing current R2/R3 state) | False claim; no AI models are currently integrated. | "Data-informed..." |
| "Revolutionary / Disruptive" | Cliché; let the results speak. | "Modernizing..." |
| "Real-time" (unless latency is < 1 second and guaranteed) | Inaccurate for most agricultural data feeds. | "Current", "Daily", "Frequent updates" |

## Claims Verification Protocol

Before any claim regarding platform capability is made externally:

1.  **Check the Status Register:** Is the capability listed as `LIVE` or `BUILT` in the `ZARATI_CAPABILITY_STATUS_REGISTER.md`?
2.  **If `PLANNED` or `VISION`:** The language must explicitly state that the feature is "on the roadmap," "in development," or "planned for future release." It must never be presented as currently functional.
3.  **Data Source Citation:** Any claims regarding market prices, weather accuracy, or farmer demographics must cite the specific, verifiable source of that data.

## Financial & Pilot Claims

*   **Do not extrapolate pilot success prematurely.** Success in Gedaref does not guarantee immediate success in Gezira.
*   **Do not project adoption curves without caveats.**
*   **Always acknowledge the operational realities** (e.g., connectivity issues, literacy rates, macroeconomic volatility) rather than painting a utopian picture.
